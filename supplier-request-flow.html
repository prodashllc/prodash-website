Request Workflow

How supplier opportunities are reviewed.

1

Supplier Inquiry

Initial message or application is received by the purchasing contact.

2

Business Review

Company identity and supplier requirements are reviewed.

3

Documentation

Requested business documents are provided during onboarding when appropriate.

4

Catalog Evaluation

Products are reviewed for brand policy, category fit, restrictions and economics.

5

Terms Review

MAP, marketplace, shipping, payment and invoice requirements are confirmed.

6

Purchase Order

Orders are placed only after terms, documentation and product eligibility are clear.
