Purchasing Policies

Responsible purchasing and supplier policy review.

ProDash LLC reviews supplier requirements, MAP policies and channel restrictions before purchasing decisions are made.

Supplier policy review

  • ✓MAP and pricing policies are reviewed.
  • ✓Marketplace and channel restrictions are respected.
  • ✓Brand authorization requirements are considered before purchasing.
  • ✓Unauthorized or unclear supply sources are avoided.

Purchase order discipline

  • ✓Orders are placed only after supplier terms are understood.
  • ✓Invoices and item details are retained for business records.
  • ✓Shipping and prep requirements are reviewed before payment.
  • ✓Long-term supplier relationships are preferred over one-time transactions.
