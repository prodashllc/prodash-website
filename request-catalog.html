Catalog access and product review process.

ProDash LLC evaluates catalogs according to supplier policy, product eligibility, pricing structure, marketplace restrictions and order economics before purchase decisions are made.

1

Catalog Access

Supplier provides approved catalog or price list access.

2

Policy Review

MAP, channel and reseller rules are reviewed first.

3

Product Match

Eligible products are matched to marketplace listings where permitted.

4

Economics

Landed cost, fees, margin and ROI are reviewed.

5

PO Decision

Orders are considered only after product and policy fit is clear.

6

Reorder

Successful items may move to structured reorder review.
