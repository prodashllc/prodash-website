Company Overview

Professional wholesale purchasing for the U.S. market.

ProDash LLC is a U.S. wholesale purchasing company focused on building supplier relationships with manufacturers, brands and authorized distributors.

Company purpose

ProDash LLC purchases consumer products through legitimate wholesale relationships for responsible online retail distribution in the United States.

Operating model

The company uses a professional purchasing process and may work with qualified third-party prep center partners when fulfillment preparation is required.

We do not position ProDash LLC as a large warehouse operator. The company is organized as a specialized wholesale purchasing business built around supplier communication, documentation and responsible sourcing.
