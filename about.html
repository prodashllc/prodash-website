Company Overview

Organized for responsible wholesale purchasing.

ProDash LLC is a U.S. limited liability company focused on wholesale purchasing, supplier communication, and responsible online retail distribution.

Supplier-first communication

Supplier policies, documentation requirements, marketplace rules, pricing requirements, and product eligibility are reviewed before purchase decisions are made.

Operating principle

ProDash LLC operates as a specialized wholesale purchasing company serving the U.S. ecommerce market through authorized supplier relationships and qualified third-party logistics partners when required.

Documentation process

Business documentation is not published publicly, but may be provided directly to approved suppliers during account onboarding when required.

Long-term intent

The company is structured for repeat purchasing relationships, not short-term retail arbitrage or unauthorized resale models.

Professional wholesale purchasing and responsible online retail distribution for the U.S. market.
