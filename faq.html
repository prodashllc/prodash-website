Supplier FAQ

Frequently asked supplier questions.

Answers to common questions from manufacturers, brands and distributors reviewing ProDash LLC.

Do you purchase wholesale?

Yes. ProDash LLC reviews wholesale purchasing opportunities with manufacturers, brands, importers and authorized distributors.

Do you sell on Amazon?

ProDash LLC may use major U.S. ecommerce marketplaces and fulfillment networks where permitted by supplier agreements and channel policies.

Do you operate a warehouse?

Inventory preparation may be handled through qualified third-party prep center partners when required. ProDash LLC does not claim to operate a proprietary warehouse network.

Can you provide resale documentation?

Applicable resale documentation is available during supplier onboarding when requested.

Can you comply with MAP policies?

Supplier pricing, MAP and channel policies are reviewed before purchasing decisions are made.

How should suppliers contact you?

Email sales@prodashllc.com for purchasing, wholesale account or catalog inquiries.
