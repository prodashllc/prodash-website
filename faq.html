Supplier FAQ

Answers for wholesale account managers.

These questions address common supplier review topics before account opening.

Do you purchase wholesale?

Yes. ProDash LLC is focused on wholesale purchasing relationships with legitimate suppliers.

Do you sell on online marketplaces?

Sales channels may include major U.S. ecommerce marketplaces where permitted by supplier agreements and product eligibility.

Do you have a sales tax documentation?

Sales tax documentation is provided during supplier onboarding when requested by approved suppliers.

Do you operate a warehouse?

ProDash LLC does not claim to operate a company-owned warehouse. Inventory preparation may be handled by qualified third-party prep center partners when required.

Can you sign supplier agreements?

Supplier agreements, MAP requirements and channel policies are reviewed before purchasing decisions.

How should suppliers contact ProDash LLC?

Email sales@prodashllc.com with wholesale account requirements, application links or catalog review instructions.
